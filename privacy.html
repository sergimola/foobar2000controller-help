foobar2000 controller — Help

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use foobar2000 controller, an Android app that controls foobar2000 on your own computer. The app does not play audio locally.

Controller and contact

The app developer responsible for this app is Sergi Mola. For privacy or support questions, email foobar2000controller@gmail.com.

Connection to your computer

The app uses the address and port you enter to connect to the Beefweb component on your computer, control the player, check its status, and display library information. This communication takes place directly between your device and the configured computer; it does not pass through a server operated by the developer.

The connection to Beefweb uses HTTP without TLS encryption. Depending on the network, others with access to its traffic may be able to observe or modify the communication. Use a trusted network and do not expose the Beefweb port directly to the internet.

Optional diagnostics in Alpha versions

Some Alpha versions may include an optional diagnostics feature. When enabled, it records technical and performance events on the device. Before saving them, the app anonymizes identifiers and replaces addresses and paths with categories; the records may include the app version, outcomes, timings, and redacted error messages.

Records are stored locally in a bounded store. You can clear them in the app settings; when the store reaches its limit, the oldest records are removed first. Sharing them does not delete the local copy.

The app does not send these diagnostics automatically. If you choose to share them, it opens an email draft with an attachment for you to review and send from your email app. The report is transmitted to the support mailbox only when you send the email. Your email provider may also process and retain the message.

Support emails and purchases

If you contact support, we will process the information you include to respond to your request. Avoid including passwords, full payment details, or other sensitive information that is not needed.

Purchases are handled through Google Play and are subject to Google's privacy practices. The app does not use its own analytics service or a server of its own to receive telemetry from distributed versions.

Retention and requests

Diagnostics that remain on the device are kept until you clear them, they are removed when the local limit is reached, or the app data is cleared. Once an email has been sent, the app cannot recall the message or its copies from the sender's, email providers', or other recipients' mailboxes.

To ask about or request deletion of a report copy received in the support mailbox, contact us. Requests will be reviewed under applicable law; a request cannot delete copies that remain under the control of other providers or recipients.

Where applicable data protection law gives you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, you can exercise them by writing to the contact address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and indicate the revision date.
