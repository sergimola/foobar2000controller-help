Connect your phone to foobar2000

  1. On the PC, install the Beefweb component in foobar2000. Open the downloaded component file and follow foobar2000's install prompt.
  2. In Beefweb's component settings, enable remote connections. Leave foobar2000 running and allow the Beefweb port through the PC's firewall on your trusted private network.
  3. On your phone, choose My PC is ready to find it, or Help me set it up for guided setup. When your PC appears, tap it to connect.

Discovery checks Beefweb's default port, 8880. If you use another port or your PC is not found, choose Enter address manually and enter the PC's network address and port. Use the PC's address on your network, not localhost.

Still can’t connect? Check that both devices are on a network that allows them to reach each other, Beefweb remote connections are enabled, and the firewall allows the configured port. Guest Wi-Fi often isolates devices.

The connection to Beefweb uses HTTP. Keep it on a trusted private network and do not expose the Beefweb port directly to the public internet.

Restore a PRO purchase

  1. In the app, open Settings and choose Upgrade to PRO.
  2. Choose Restore purchase.
  3. Make sure Google Play is signed in to the account that made the purchase.
  4. If PRO still does not appear, email app support privately. Do not post order numbers, receipts, or payment details in a public forum.

Google Play handles payment and refund requests. Read Google Play’s refund information or contact the app developer through Google Play.

Server sign-in is not supported yet

This app cannot connect to Beefweb servers that require a username and password.

Contact app support

For setup or app-specific problems, email foobar2000controller@gmail.com. Include your phone model, Android version, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Never send passwords, full payment details, or purchase receipts.
